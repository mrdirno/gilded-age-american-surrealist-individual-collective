## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a minor character—a porter, a factory worker, a socialite—feels their individual identity being subtly pulled into the Prism's collective impression.
- Describe a new 'echo' or byproduct of the Consortium's technology: a surreal photograph or event that Silas discovers.
- Create a journal entry from Miss Vickers detailing her true, personal opinion on the 'spirit of the age' she is helping to entrap.
- Write a chapter set 50 years after the novel's end, describing how the Prism on Fifth Avenue has evolved or decayed, and what collective memory it now holds.

### Technical Entry Points

- Design a visual 'mood board' or architectural schematic for Silas's Prism interior, using surrealist art and Gilded Age design as references.
- Develop a system for 'mapping' the collective emotions/ambitions of a historical period onto a physical, navigable 3D space, defining its rules and distortions.
